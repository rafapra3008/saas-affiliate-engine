Main use cases

The strength of this tool lies in its ability to connect typically separate marketing steps into one cohesive flow.

  • Lead Generation Funnels: Rapidly build professional landing pages, capture leads, and immediately enroll them into automated email sequences—all within the same interface.
  • Automated Customer Journeys: Design complex, trigger-based workflows (e.g., if X happens, wait 3 days, send email Y, if opened, send offer Z).

  • Webinars and Events: Host and promote webinars, then automatically follow up with registrants (attendees vs. no-shows) to maximize conversion opportunities.
