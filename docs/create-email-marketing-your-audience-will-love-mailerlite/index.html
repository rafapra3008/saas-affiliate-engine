Pricing Overview

MailerLite’s pricing model is one of its strongest selling points, offering tremendous value for growing businesses.

They operate on a tiered system primarily based on your subscriber count, ensuring the cost scales affordably as you grow.

The Free Plan: MailerLite offers a perpetually free plan, suitable for users with up to 1,000 subscribers. This plan includes most core features, such as the drag-and-drop editor, the website builder, and basic automations, allowing you to establish your foundation completely risk-free.

Paid Plans: Paid tiers unlock unlimited monthly sending, advanced features (like removal of the MailerLite logo, A/B testing, and comprehensive custom segmentation), and dedicated support. Pricing remains competitive and transparent as your list expands.
